# ANR and DVD voice architecture

ANR and DVD are treated as grounded research displays. Voice may choose documented fields and an exact ISO history range, but nested runtime control remains disabled until Godel's live callbacks and rendered postconditions are verified.

## ANR

The exact field vocabulary separates consensus rating and consensus price target from row-level firm, analyst, rating, price target, date, and upgrade/downgrade values. A history request needs explicit `from` and `to` ISO dates; phrases such as “last year” are not converted using an assumed clock.

Narration requires a current `Godel ANR panel` snapshot containing its as-of date, exact consensus values, currency, and exact live rows. Analyst price targets are reported as attributed observations—not as forecasts produced by Jarvis. Jarvis does not calculate implied upside, infer buy/sell advice, or extrapolate a missing consensus.

ANR's export control is documented, but its output format is not. Export therefore remains format-unresolved and disabled even when the user says CSV, Excel, or JSON.

## DVD

The documented field vocabulary includes 12-month and indicated yield, one- and three-year growth, payment frequency, yield history, declaration/ex/record/payable dates, amount, and dividend type. Exact ISO history ranges are modeled; relative date ranges clarify.

Narration requires exact current `Godel DVD panel` summary fields and dividend rows. A future-dated row may be read only if it is already displayed and declared by Godel. Jarvis never predicts a dividend amount, ex-date, payable date, future yield, or growth rate.

Corrections replace the superseded clause. Uncorrected field/date conflicts, unsupported inference, missing grounding, or export ambiguity block the whole compound result.
