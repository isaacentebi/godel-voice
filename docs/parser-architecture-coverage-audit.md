# Parser and architecture coverage audit

The machine-readable audit in `data/parser-architecture-coverage-v1.json` joins all 59 canonical commands to four honest states: live, strict-unbound, safety-gated, or open-only. “Live” is limited to bindings currently enabled in the adapter registry; a parser or successful offline test alone does not qualify.

After the Q/HLDR/HMS and MOSO/CITADEL/ERR closure passes, no canonical command remains dependent only on generic catalog knowledge. Open-only live-undocumented surfaces still state their limitations explicitly, and ERR remains safety-gated because it submits information externally. Aliases are reconciled to the canonical registry: TAS/TR, N/CN/NH, SECF/SEARCH/TK, OMON/OPT/CALL/PUT, and G/GIP/GP.

DES was selected as the highest-value remaining read-only family because it is a common company-research hub and carries current valuation, ownership, capital structure, dividend, beta and short-interest facts plus six documented handoffs. Its new strict architecture is grounded-only and runtime-disabled.
